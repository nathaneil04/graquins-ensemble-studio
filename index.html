Why This Firm?

Market / Client Focus

We believe when passion, expertise and innovation combine we can create amazing experiences and a better world together. This belief drives us when designing your own version of living.

1.

Small-Scale Residential Projects

Focusing on high-impact design for compact living, tailored interior renovations, home additions, and custom single-family dwellings. We maximize usable footprint, refine interior flow, and integrate personalized details that elevate everyday home life within tighter spatial constraints.

2.

Medium-Scale Residential Projects

Crafting bespoke multi-story residences, luxury family homes, and low-density multi-unit developments. From conceptual site integration to structural coordination, we design cohesive, enduring living environments scaled to support growing lifestyle needs and context-sensitive site conditions.

3.

Commercial Spaces and Institutional Projects

Delivering functional, brand-aligned environments for boutique retail shops, dining establishments, modern office interiors, and community-focused facilities. We balance local building codes, public accessibility, and operational workflows to build engaging spaces designed for business growth and civic utility.

Governance Framework

Authority & Reporting

Defined decision-making boundaries for autonomous action versus required principal sign-offs.

Authority

Decisions subordinates may make independently within their assigned roles:

  • Manage CAD/BIM layer setups, templates, and block libraries.
  • Conduct routine coordination meetings with MEPF and other technical consultants.
  • Prepare initial drafts of specifications and material schedules for review.
  • Conduct routine site walkthroughs and document progress and observations.
  • Manage day-to-day project coordination and scheduling within the approved project scope.

Approval

Decisions requiring Principal Architect review and sign-off:

  • Any change to the approved design concept, building footprint, or major spatial configuration.
  • Final signing and sealing of official architectural documents.
  • Approval and signing of contracts, professional fee proposals, and client variations.
  • Major changes affecting project scope, budget, schedule, or design intent.
  • Resolution of major site conflicts, client disputes, or contractual issues.
